---
name: assurance-review
description: Review explicitly scoped security, AI safety and compliance evidence using a project's pinned AI Assurance profile. Use for assurance reviews or claim verification, not routine implementation or legal certification.
---

Read the project's `.ai-assurance/AGENTS.md`, profile and selected requirements.
Use only evidence the user authorized for that scope. Validate the pinned
snapshot, then generate a report with the bundled CLI. The CLI does not execute
evidence methods. Keep declared and simulated material separate from executed
checks. Record unresolved evidence and proposed follow-up tasks in the project's
local tracker. Human acceptance does not change evidence origin. Do not report
certification, whole-framework conformity or model consensus without its own
observed evidence.
